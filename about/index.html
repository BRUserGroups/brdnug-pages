Baton Rouge .NET User Group

A free, no-registration-required user group for professionals and students in the broad field of .NET development — meeting monthly in Baton Rouge.

Who we are

The Baton Rouge .NET User Group (BRDNUG.org) is a free, no-registration-required user group for professionals and students in the broad field of .NET development. The group aims to provide a platform for growing your skillset in Information Technology and your professional network, and works closely with local and regional partners to make our monthly meetings interesting and informative.

We meet every second Wednesday of the month on a wide range of topics including C#, ASP.NET, Azure, DevOps, and modern application architecture.

The goal is to help the Baton Rouge area .NET community become more involved, educated, and capable technology professionals.

Partner groups

Meetings are held monthly in conjunction with our partner user groups:

  • Baton Rouge Analytics & Intelligence Network — gobrain.org
  • Baton Rouge SQL Server User Group — BRSSUG.org

Not-for-profit corporation

BRDNUG operates under Baton Rouge User Groups, a Louisiana not-for-profit corporation (501c6) formed to support all user groups and user group events with financial logistics. Baton Rouge User Groups has supported the SQL and .NET user groups as well as Day of Data events. Our EIN and a W-9 are available upon request.

Day of Data Baton Rouge

BRDNUG is part of an annual full-day data conference — free sessions, great speakers, and the Baton Rouge data community all in one place. For more information on speaking, volunteering, or attending, visit dayofdatabr.org.
