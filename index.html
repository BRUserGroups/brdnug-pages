About

What is BRDNUG?

The Baton Rouge .NET User Group has been connecting local technology professionals for years. We're a free, community-run group focused on C#, ASP.NET, Azure, and the broader .NET ecosystem.

Meetings feature presentations from local experts and Microsoft MVPs, followed by open discussion and networking. Whether you're a seasoned architect, a junior developer, or just getting started — you're welcome here.
